## Design Context

### Users
Students and board-exam reviewers (nursing, LET, engineering, CPA) who sit with PDFs, lecture notes, and messy highlights. They use Comtus to ground chat in their own sources, drill quizzes, and polish notes — often late, often for long stretches, often with the tab left open.

### Brand Personality
Warm scholar. Crafted, lamplit, precise. Three words: **warm, scholarly, alive**. The interface should feel like a library at midnight with one lamp still burning — not a hype startup, not a grayscale productivity tool, and not a cartoon game. Copy stays second person, calm, no exclamation marks.

### Aesthetic Direction
**Precision instrument console** (locked design direction). Cantor8 royal sapphire on the marketing surface; a calibrated study desk in the app. Landing: continuous spectacle — Cantor8 sapphire console chrome, circuit streams, pixel-matrix dissolves, telemetry grid — with Pixelify Sans Variable as display accent only. Studio: fast and tactile; typography comes from the curated four-preset system (Instrument default, Pixel Signal, Scholar, Console — see `src/lib/fontPresets.ts`), with pixel chrome (brass-bevel palette, ink-press physics) as a separate Desk-chrome toggle. Help and legal pages stay calm. Motifs: the beam (light as understanding) and the margin (scholar's dialogue with text). Anti-references: cyan/purple AI slop, Duolingo-style XP shouting, glassmorphism, neon-on-black, unreadable 8-bit display faces (never Press Start 2P), Pixelify as body text. Color beyond neutrals stays in-family: brass (primary), moon (secondary), sage (success), rust (error). Life comes from calibrated glows, beam choreography, ink-press tactility, and a quietly reactive battery companion — not rainbow chrome.

### Design Principles
1. Color carries meaning (tool kind, state, milestone accent) and warmth — never decoration for its own sake.
2. Delight is skippable, muted, and on-brand: a corner battery that assists, not a mascot that blocks study.
3. Achievements are study craft ("the first page on the desk"), never streaks-as-guilt or XP.
4. Motion is light but lively: flicker, bloom, stepped hops, arcade press. `prefers-reduced-motion` rests ambient loops; SFX is a separate mute. Typography is a curated preset system (Settings → Typography); pixel chrome is a separate Desk-look toggle. The marketing landing is the spectacle surface; help and legal pages stay calm.
5. Local-first: milestone progress is derived from the on-device library, stored beside settings.
