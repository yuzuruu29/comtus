## Design Context

### Users
Students and board-exam reviewers (nursing, LET, engineering, CPA) who sit with PDFs, lecture notes, and messy highlights. They use Comtus to ground chat in their own sources, drill quizzes, and polish notes — often late, often for long stretches, often with the tab left open.

### Brand Personality
Warm scholar. Crafted, lamplit, precise. Three words: **warm, scholarly, alive**. The interface should feel like a library at midnight with one lamp still burning — not a hype startup, not a grayscale productivity tool, and not a cartoon game. Copy stays second person, calm, no exclamation marks.

### Aesthetic Direction
**Lamplight Study** (dark default) and **Daylight Study** (app-only). Brass on ink-blue. Landing: full Pixelify Sans Variable (headlines, nav, body, captions, chrome) with brass grid, scanlines, stepped hops, and arcade press — midnight library, lamp-arcade overlay. Studio Pixel desk (default): Pixelify Sans Variable on every desk line (chat, notes, markdown, settings, composer, buttons) with arcade press and brass grid. Scholar restores Fraunces + Inter in the app only. Motifs: the lamp (light as understanding) and the margin (scholar's dialogue with text). Anti-references: cyan/purple AI slop, Duolingo-style XP shouting, glassmorphism, neon-on-black, unreadable 8-bit display faces (never Press Start 2P). Color beyond neutrals stays in-family: brass (primary), moon (secondary), sage (success), rust (error). Life comes from warmer glows, a brass grid, stepped lamp hops, arcade card press, a pokeable pixel lamp, and a pixel lamp companion — not rainbow chrome.

### Design Principles
1. Color carries meaning (tool kind, state, milestone accent) and warmth — never decoration for its own sake.
2. Delight is skippable, muted, and on-brand: a corner lamp that cheers, not a mascot that blocks study.
3. Achievements are study craft ("the first page on the desk"), never streaks-as-guilt or XP.
4. Motion is light but lively: flicker, bloom, stepped hops, arcade press. `prefers-reduced-motion` rests ambient loops; SFX is a separate mute. Pixel vs Scholar is a studio Settings toggle; the marketing landing is always pixel.
5. Local-first: milestone progress is derived from the on-device library, stored beside settings.
