The Comtus Learning Loop: Understand, Organize, Practice, Master

  1. 1. Understand — Clear explanations, step-by-step logic, intuitive analogies, and concept breakdowns suited to your current level.
  2. 2. Organize — Transform messy lecture slides, textbook PDFs, and typed notes into structured summaries, outlines, and topic study guides.
  3. 3. Practice — Active recall testing with customizable quizzes, flashcards, and full-length simulated mock exams with detailed answer rationales.
  4. 4. Master — Identify knowledge gaps, target weak topics, and build long-term retention before your exams.

Why Comtus Instead of a Generic Chatbot?

Generic chatbots give one-off answers in temporary chat logs. Comtus is a purposeful study workspace built for active learning. Every answer cites your source pages, thinking is shown openly, and study materials stay organized per subject.

Academic Integrity & Responsible AI

Comtus is built to support learning — not replace it. We emphasize guided questioning, worked examples, hint progression, and transparent citations so students master concepts on their own.

Local-First Privacy & Data Ownership

Your notes, PDFs, and study sessions remain on your device in local browser storage. Your study materials are never sold or used for model training.
